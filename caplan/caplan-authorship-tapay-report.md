# Authorship in Caplan's lab + UAlberta undergrad TA pay — Research Report

Prepared 2026-10-18. Research only. Nothing drafted, sent, or contacted.

## Q1 — Do undergrads in Caplan's lab actually get authorship?

**Verdict: Yes — regularly, and not just acknowledgement-only. On 11 papers from 2020–2026 (incl. one in press), a co-author was an undergrad in his lab, including 5 first-author papers. The catch is lag: most papers appear 1–6 years after the student's undergrad tenure ended, so authorship reflects work done as an undergrad but published later.**

Source: Caplan's CV dated Oct 17, 2026 (http://www.psych.ualberta.ca/~cml/JBC/CV_Jeremy_Caplan.pdf), cross-referencing its Publications list against its Supervision → Undergraduate students list. (Caveat: the CV says trainees are underlined, but the text extraction loses underlining, so matches were made by name against the supervision list. **Pattern:**
- Undergrads appear as **first authors (5 of the 11)** and as 2nd/3rd authors — not acknowledgement-only. First authorships are typically the student's honours-thesis project written up for publication.
- **Lag is the norm, not the exception.** Only 3 of the 11 were published during the student's tenure (Pawluk 2025; Shafaghat Ardebili 2022; Kluger et al. 2022). The rest appeared 1–6 years later. Implication: a Winter 2027 RA stint would realistically yield a publication in 2028–2031, if at all — not during the term.
- **The recent CS undergrads (Kadoura 2023–2026, Anquour 2024–2025, Jutt 2023–2025) have no authorships yet** — consistent with the lag, not evidence of exclusion.
- Authorship follows **credit-course work too**: most of these students did PSYCH 299/396/398/496/498 independent-study courses, and still got authorship. Credit vs. pay does not determine authorship; the work does.

**Gaps / what would settle it:** The CV does not state when each paper's work was done, so "work done as undergrad, published later" is an inference from tenure dates + author overlap, not a CV-stated fact. What would settle it: the papers' own acknowledgements/methods (e.g., "based on the first author's honours thesis"), or asking Caplan directly. The CV also cannot show acknowledgement-only cases — if some undergrads got only acknowledgements, that is invisible here. The Tan (in press, 2026) case is genuinely ambiguous on timing.

## Q2 — What does a UAlberta undergraduate TA actually get paid?

**Verdict: No published UAlberta undergraduate TA hourly rate was found.** Four searches (CS TA posting info, UAlberta HR / NASA pages, Graduate Student Assistantship Collective Agreement) turned up no official undergraduate TA rate. Do not treat any figure below as UAlberta's rate.

**What the sources actually establish:**
- **Graduate TAs are covered by the Graduate Student Assistantship Collective Agreement** (UAlberta Nursing / KSR pages confirm: "All graduate assistantships, student markers, and proctors are governed by the Graduate Student Assistantship Collective Agreement"). That agreement covers **graduate students only** — it does not set undergraduate TA pay.
- **Undergraduate student employment floors (UAlberta URI hire page, https://www.ualberta.ca/en/current-students/undergraduate-research-initiative/hire-undergraduate-researchers.html):** excluded student employees must be paid at least Alberta minimum wage, **$15.00/hr** + mandatory employment-related costs; non-excluded positions must pay at least NASA Salary Scale Grade 1, Step 1 (the dollar figure is **not** stated on that page). These are floors for undergraduate student employment generally, not a TA rate.
- **Third-party estimates only (not official, not undergrad-specific):** Indeed self-reported data puts a UAlberta Teaching Assistant at ~**$21.19–$21.47/hr** (8–12 self-reported salaries, mixing graduate and undergraduate TAs). This is corroboration at best — it is not UAlberta's published rate and does not separate undergrad from grad TAs.
- **Not UAlberta:** a federal database PDF showing graduate-assistant rates of ~$38–$48/hr is a different institution's agreement — not cited as UAlberta's.

**What would settle it:** the rate is set in the CS department's TA offer/contract (the application went through csugrad@ualberta.ca). The only authoritative source is the department or the offer letter itself.

## Bottom line for the decision

- **Authorship in Caplan's lab is real and recurring** — undergrads, including a CS undergrad on a modelling paper, have been first/second authors. But it is a **long game**: expect 1–6 years from work to publication, so it is leverage for grad school / later applications, not short-term income or a Winter 2027 deliverable.
- **TA pay has no published UAlberta undergraduate rate**; the only verified anchors are the $15/hr student-employment floor and an unofficial ~$21/hr self-reported average. Neither the RA band ($17–$20/hr, from the earlier URI-based research) nor the TA figure is verifiable as "official" — so a claim that one pays more than the other cannot be grounded in published rates.
