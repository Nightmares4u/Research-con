**Does the grant type permit paying undergrad RAs? **Direct grant-funded paid RA (from Caplan's Discovery Grant) — the viable route.** No application cycle, no deadline. Caplan decides rate, hours, and duties. This is the only route that fits a part-time Winter-term RA.

**NSERC USRA — CLOSED for Winter 2027, on two independent grounds:**
1. **Deadline passed.** UAlberta runs one USRA cycle per year. The deadline covering Winter 2027 awards was **February 2, 2026** — before Yashal ever contacted Caplan. The next cycle (deadline February 2027, TBA) is for Summer 2027 / Fall 2027 / Winter 2028.
2. **Full-time rule.** A USRA is 16 consecutive weeks at ~35 hrs/week, and holders may not be enrolled in courses during tenure. That is incompatible with a part-time Winter RA alongside coursework. (UAlberta does allow USRAs to be *tenable* in Winter term — but only via that missed cycle, full-time.)
- Two further checks if USRA ever comes up again: it requires Canadian citizenship / PR / Protected Person status (Yashal's status not verified in this research — flag, don't assume), and the supervisor must hold an active NSERC grant — Caplan qualifies.

**URI internal funding (URI Stipend, Alberta Innovates SRS, etc.) — not a Winter route.** The URI streamlined competition is summer-focused; no Winter-term URI stipend found. The Faculty of Education's Winter research award is restricted to Education students and is not applicable.

**Course credit (fallback only):** PSYCH 299/396/398/496/498 or CMPUT 499 exist, but **credit ≠ pay**, and a CS student doing credit research in a Psych lab needs cross-department approval. Per Yashal's priorities, this is second-tier.
